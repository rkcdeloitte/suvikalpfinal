Second-Party Opinion (SPO) Providers

A Second-Party Opinion is an independent assessment of a green, social, sustainability, or sustainability-linked financing framework against recognised market principles — such as the ICMA Green Bond Principles or the Green Loan Principles. Getting an SPO is a key credibility step before taking a green bond or green loan to market. Below is a directory of recognised SPO providers, both global and India-focused.

Note: in India, entities offering ESG ratings and second-party opinions on debt instruments may also be registered with SEBI as an ESG Rating Provider (ERP) — this carries additional regulatory weight for official disclosures.
